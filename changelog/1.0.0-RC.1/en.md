First release candidate of Vixeeny 1.0. Please report anything that goes wrong: Settings → About → Report a problem.

## Captures

- Captures can be saved smaller, at 75 %, 50 % or 25 % of their size (Settings → Image → Saved size). The file and the clipboard both get the smaller picture.
- Each kind of capture can wait 3, 5 or 10 seconds before it is taken (Delay before the capture). Pressing its shortcut again during the wait cancels it.
- New shortcut: Capture the last region again. It saves the region last copied or saved from the editor straight to a file, without the editor.
- Pin: a new editor button (CTRL + P) keeps the capture above the other windows, where it was taken. Drag it to move it, scroll to zoom, right-click to copy or close it, double-click or press Escape to close it.

## Recording

- New shortcuts: Record a region and Record a window. Pick the region or the window to record; the same shortcut stops the recording.
- The new shortcuts have no key by default: set one in Settings → Shortcuts.

## Changes

- The editor's tooltips write their shortcuts as the rest of Vixeeny does (CTRL + SHIFT + S).
